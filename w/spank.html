    spank

    /spæŋk/ やや難 頻度 10771位

    意味

    1. (罰として平手などで)…のしりをピシャリと打つ
    2. (しりへの)平手打ち

    例文

    • If you do that again, I'll spank you.

      次やったら、お尻ペンペンしますよ。

      Tatoeba #10546828

    • When I was a child, I was spanked if I did something wrong.

      子どもの頃、悪さをするといつも尻を叩かれた。

      Tatoeba #541766

    • When I was a child, whenever I did something bad, I would get spanked.

      子どもの頃、悪さをするといつもお尻を叩かれた。

      Tatoeba #2658392

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Uncertain. Possibly imitative, or perhaps a special use of Etymology 2 (below) in the sense of a horse stamping its foot against the ground. There is no evidence of a relation with Portuguese espancar (“to batter, strike”).
