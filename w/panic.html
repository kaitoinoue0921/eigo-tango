    panic

    /pˈænɪk/ 頻出 頻度 2459位

    要点あわてふためき,恐慌

    意味

    1. あわてふためき,恐慌,ろうばい
    2. 経済恐慌,パニック
    3. 《単数形で》《米俗》とてもおかしなもの,珍無類なもの
    4. あわてふためく,恐慌に陥る
    5. …をあわてさせる
    6. 《米俗》〈観客〉を沸きたたせる

    例文

    語源

    The adjective is borrowed from Middle French panique, a word itself borrowed from Ancient Greek πανικός (panikós, “pertaining to Pan”); Pan, the Greek god of fields and woods, was believed to be the source of mysterious sounds that caused contagious, groundless fear in herds and crowds, or in people in lonely spots. Adjective sense 3 (“pertaining to or resulting from overpowering fear or fright”) is partly an attributive use of the noun. The noun is derived from the adjective, while the verb is derived from the noun.
