    plunder

    /plˈʌndɚ/ やや難 頻度 11112位

    要点略奪して荒らす

    意味

    1. 〈村・町・国など〉を略奪して荒らす;(…から)…を略奪する《+名+of+名》
    2. 略奪する
    3. 略奪品
    4. 略奪すること

    語源

    Recorded since 1632 during the Thirty Years War, native British use since the Cromwellian Civil War. Borrowed from German plündern (“to loot”), from Middle High German, from Middle Low German plunderen, from a noun originally meaning "household goods, bedding, clothing," of obscure ultimate origin. This is first attested in medieval records, and according to Gijsseling, is therefore attested too late to be considered a substrate word. Due to the lack of obvious cognates in other languages from which it would have been loaned, it could have developed as some slang word in Lower Saxony/the Low Countries. Cognate with Dutch plunderen, West Frisian plonderje, Saterland Frisian plunnerje.
