    muffin

    /mˈʌfən/ やや難 頻度 7183位

    意味

    1. マッフィン
    2. 《英》円形の軽焼きパン;熱いうちにバターを付けて食べる(《米》English muffin)
    3. 《米》バターを付けて食べるロール(カップ)ケーキ

    例文

    • How many blueberry muffins did you eat yesterday?

      ブルーベリーマフィン、昨日幾つ食べたの？

      Tatoeba #11149263

    • I don't know why I need to stop eating muffins.

      どうしてマフィンを食べるの止めなくちゃいけないのか、それが分かんないんだ。

      Tatoeba #9125416

    • There's only blueberry muffins for breakfast!

      朝は、ブルーベリーマフィンに限る！

      Tatoeba #12589041

    語源

    From earlier moofin, probably a diminutive of Low German Muffe ("small cake"; Muffen (plural)), from Middle Low German muffe (“small pastry”). An alternative, and phonologically less plausible theory suggests a connection to Old French (pain) moflet (“roll”, literally “soft (bread)”). Sense “vulva” influenced by muff.
