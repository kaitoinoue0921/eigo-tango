    worm

    /wɚm/ 標準 頻度 4889位

    要点虫

    意味

    1. (細長く柔らかい)虫(毛虫・うじ虫・ミミズ・回虫など)
    2. (形・動きが)ミミズなどに似たもの,(いも虫状の)ねじのらせん
    3. 虫けらのような人間;哀れな(下劣な)人
    4. 《複数形で;単数扱い》寄生虫病
    5. (虫のように)体をくねらせて進む
    6. …から寄生虫を駆除する

    例文

    • Some people say stepping on a worm makes it rain.

      ミミズを踏むと雨が降るという人がいる。

      Tatoeba #32233

    • The mother bird brought worms for her young ones.

      母鳥が雛に虫を持ってきた。

      Tatoeba #320946

    • Even the smallest worm will turn if trodden on.

      極小の虫でも踏まれれば、そりくり返る。

      Tatoeba #18862

    語源

    Etymology tree Proto-Germanic *wurmiz Proto-West Germanic *wurmi Old English wyrm Middle English worm English worm From Middle English werm, wirm, worm, worme, wrim, wurm, wyrm, wyrme, from Old English weorm, wurm, wyrm, from Proto-West Germanic *wurmi, from Proto-Germanic *wurmiz (“worm; snake”), from Proto-Indo-European *wr̥mis (“worm; larva”), possibly from *wer- (“to turn”). Doublet of vermin and wyrm, the latter of which is a fairly recent borrowing directly from the Old English. (computing): First computer usage by John Brunner in his 1975 book The Shockwave Rider.
