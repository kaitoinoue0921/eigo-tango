    threat

    /θrɛt/ 頻出 頻度 2076位

    要点脅し、きざし

    意味

    1. 脅し,脅迫
    2. (…の)(悪い)きざし,前兆《+of+名(doing)》
    3. (…にとって)危険な人(もの);不幸(災難)のもと《+to+名》

    例文

    • I had to resort to threats to get my money back.

      金を返してもらうために、ついに脅してやった。

      Tatoeba #18552

    • Threats of retaliation are blocking negotiations.

      報復のおどかしが交渉を妨げています。

      Tatoeba #320980

    • Air pollution will be a threat to our survival.

      大気汚染は我々の生存を脅かすものになるだろう。

      Tatoeba #275656

    語源

    From Middle English threte, thret, thrat, thræt, threat, from Old English þrēat (“crowd, swarm, troop, army, press; pressure, trouble, calamity, oppression, force, violence, threat”), from Proto-Germanic *þrautaz, closely tied to Proto-Germanic *þrautą (“displeasure, complaint, grievance, labour, toil”), from Proto-Indo-European *trewd- (“to squeeze, push, press”).
