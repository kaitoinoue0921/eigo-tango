    eyebrow

    /ˈaɪbrˌaʊ/ やや難 頻度 10533位

    要点まゆ

    意味

    1. 眉弓(びきゅう)(目の上の弓状突起部)
    2. まゆ,まゆ毛

    例文

    • He showed his disapproval by raising an eyebrow.

      彼はまゆを上げて難色を示した。

      Tatoeba #292942

    • What the heck is the point of eyebrows anyway?

      眉毛って一体何のためにあるんだろう？

      Tatoeba #4635487

    • He really does have such thick, imposing eyebrows.

      やっぱり太めの凛々しい眉毛だね。

      Tatoeba #13614721

    語源

    From Middle English eȝebreu, alteration of Old English ēaganbrū (“eyebrow”), equivalent to eye + brow. The corresponding Old English ēagbrǣw meant "eyelid". Compare Dutch oogbrauw (“eyelid; eyelash; eyebrow”), German Augenbraue (“eyebrow”).
