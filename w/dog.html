    dogドッグ

    /dɔɡ/ 最頻出 頻度 205位

    意味

    1. 犬
    2. 雄犬;イヌ科の動物(特にイタチ・オオカミ・カワウソ)の雄
    3. 《話》《形容詞を伴って》やつ
    4. 《話》くだらない男,下劣なやつ
    5. 《米俗》粗悪な物,くだらない物
    6. つかみ道具(氷づかみ,掛けかぎなど)
    7. 《the dogs》《英》競犬
    8. (犬のように)…の後をつける,に付きまとう

    Etymology tree Old English [Term?]? Proto-Germanic *-gô Proto-West Germanic *-gō Old English -ga Old English dogga Middle English dogge English dog Inherited from Middle English dogge (akin to Scots dug), from Old English dogga, docga, of uncertain origin. The original meaning seems to have been a common dog, as opposed to a well-bred one, or something like 'cur', and perhaps later came to be used for stocky dogs. Possibly a pet-form diminutive with suffix -ga (compare frocga (“frog”), *picga (“pig”)), appended to a base *dog-, *doc- of unclear origin and meaning. One possibility is Old English dox (“dark, swarthy”) (compare frocga from frox).
