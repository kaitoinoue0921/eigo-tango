    are

    /ɑr/ 最頻出 頻度 23位

    要点である

    意味

    1. …である,…です(beの二人称の単数とすべての人称の複数の直説法現在形)
    2. アール(メートル法の面積単位;100平方メートル)

    例文

    • When you read, think about what you are reading.

      何か読むときは読んでいる物のことを考えなさい。

      Tatoeba #13265295

    • Don't look down on others because they are poor.

      貧しいことを理由に人を軽蔑してはいけない。

      Tatoeba #318509

    • We are in for an unusually hot summer this year.

      私たちは今年は例年になく暑い夏を経験しそうだ。

      Tatoeba #248710

    語源

    Etymology tree Proto-Germanic *arun? Old English earon Middle English aren English are From Middle English aren, from Old English (Anglian) earun, earon (“are”, plural). Possibly reinforced by the Old Norse plural forms in er-, this displaced the alternative forms Old English sind and bēoþ. In the second person singular it displaced archaic art. Further etymology controversial: * The English forms, as well as the Old Swedish forms in ær-, could reflect Proto-Germanic preterite-present *ōr ~ *arum, from Proto-Indo-European *h₃e-h₃ór-h₂e (“I have risen”, perfect).
