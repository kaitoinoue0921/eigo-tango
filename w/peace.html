    peace

    /pis/ 最頻出 頻度 807位

    要点平和、講和、条約、治安

    意味

    1. 平和
    2. 《しばしばP-》講和[条約]
    3. 《通例the ~》(社会の)治安,秩序
    4. (場所の)静けさ,穏やかさ
    5. (心の)静けさ,穏やかさ

    例文

    • People all over the world are anxious for peace.

      世界中の人々が平和を切望している。

      Tatoeba #271187

    • Those people contributed greatly to world peace.

      それらの人々は世界平和に大いに貢献した。

      Tatoeba #41984

    • Are we ever going to make peace with each other?

      僕らもう仲直りできないのかな？

      Tatoeba #10450347

    語源

    Etymology tree Proto-Indo-European *peh₂ḱ- Proto-Indo-European *-s Proto-Indo-European *péh₂ḱs Proto-Italic *pāks Latin pāx Old French pais Anglo-Norman peisbor. Middle English pees English peace From Middle English pees, pes, pais, borrowed from Anglo-Norman peis and Old French pais (“peace”), from Latin pāx (“peace”), from Proto-Indo-European *peh₂ḱ- (“to fasten, stick, place”), related to Latin pacīscor (“agree, stipulate”), Latin pangō (“fasten, fix”); see pact. Doublet of pax. Displaced native Old English sibb, frēols, and broadly ousted friþ (modern frith).
