    turquoise

    /tˈɚkwɔɪz/ やや難 頻度 11656位

    意味

    1. トルコ石(青緑色の準宝石)
    2. 青緑色
    3. 青緑色の

    語源

    From Middle French turquoise, from Old French (pierre) turquoise (“Turkish (stone)”), from turc + -ois. The stone, mined near Nishapur in the Khorasan region of Persia, was originally brought to Europe through Turkey. Doublet of Turkish. Compare typologically copper (<< Ancient Greek Κύπρος (Kúpros, “Cyprus”)).
