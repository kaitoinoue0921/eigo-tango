    balloon

    /bəlˈun/ 標準 頻度 3194位

    要点気球

    意味

    1. 気球,軽気球
    2. (おもちゃの)風船
    3. (気球のように)ふくれる《+out(up)》
    4. (気球のように)ふくれた

    例文

    • Yanni threw a water balloon at the math teacher.

      ヤニーたら、数学の先生に水風船を投げつけたのよ。

      Tatoeba #9958612

    • The balloon was carried away somewhere by the wind.

      その風船は風でどこかに運び去られた。

      Tatoeba #44173

    • "Did you hear that?" "It's a water balloon."

      「今の音聞こえた？」「水風船だよ」

      Tatoeba #9958604

    Etymology tree Proto-Indo-European *bʰel-der. Proto-Germanic *balluz Frankish *ballubor. Medieval Latin balla Italian palla Proto-Indo-European *-ōder. Proto-Indo-European *-Hōder.? Latin -ōnem Italian -one Italian pallonebor. ▲ Italian pallonebor. Middle French ballonbor.? English balloon First use appears c. 1591, "a game played with a large, inflated leather ball" (possibly via Middle French ballon) from Italian pallone (“large ball”) from palla (“ball”), from Lombardic *palla. The Northern Italian form, balla (“ball-shaped bundle”), today a doublet, likely derived from Old French balle, from Frankish *balla (“ball”), and may have influenced the spelling of this word.
