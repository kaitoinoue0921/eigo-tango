    要点飲み物

    意味

    1. 《文》飲み物,飲料(drink)

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English beverage, from Old French beverage, variant of bevrage, from beivre (“to drink”), variant of boivre (“to drink”), from Latin bibō. Related to imbibe.
