    footage

    /fˈʊtɪdʒ/ 標準 頻度 4984位

    意味

    1. (映画フィルム・木材などの)フィート数,全長
    2. (映画の)場面

    例文

    • They will figure out the location from the surveillance footage.

      監視カメラの映像から位置を割り出す。

      Tatoeba #11560769

    • If you look at the security camera footage closely, there's a person there.

      防犯カメラの映像をよく見たら人がいた。

      Tatoeba #11772410

    • The footage from that accident was so horrifying that it made me want to look away.

      その事故の映像は思わず目をそむけたくなるほど凄惨なものだった。

      Tatoeba #9987677

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From foot (unit of length) + -age. Video footage is so called because movie film and television tape was measured in feet. The same term is still applied by analogy to digital videos which are stored on hard drives or microchips. Compare typologically French métrage.
