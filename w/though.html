    though

    /ðoʊ/ 最頻出 頻度 585位

    要点…にもかかわらず、たとえ…でも、もっとも…ではあるが

    意味

    1. …にもかかわらず,…だけれども(although)
    2. たとえ…でも
    3. 《補足的に主節の後に置いて》もっとも…ではあるが

    例文

    • Even though they love each other, they broke up.

      彼らは好き同士なのに別れました。

      Tatoeba #1215649

    • Even though it's small, it's a great restaurant.

      小さいながらも素晴らしいレストランなんです。

      Tatoeba #1808147

    • They didn't stop working though they were tired.

      彼らは疲れていたけれども、仕事をやめなかった。

      Tatoeba #307457

Proto-Germanic *-hw Proto-Germanic *þauh Proto-West Germanic *þauh Old English þēah ▲ Proto-Germanic *þauh Old Norse *þóhder. Middle English thogh English though From Middle English though, thogh, from Old Norse *þóh (later þó). Superseded (in most dialects) Middle English thegh, from Old English þēah (“though, although, even if, that, however, nevertheless, yet, still; whether”). Both the Old Norse and Old English are from Proto-Germanic *þauh (“though”), from Proto-Indo-European *to-, suffixed with Proto-Germanic *-hw < Proto-Indo-European *-kʷe (“and”).
