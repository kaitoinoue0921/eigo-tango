    virtual

    /vˈɚtʃuəl/ 標準 頻度 4668位

    意味

    1. (名目上・表面上はそうではないが)事実上の,実際上の

    例文

    • I couldn't find my non-virtual address book until only a few minutes ago.

      非ヴァーチャルアドレス帳を数分前に見つけたところです。

      Tatoeba #317931

    語源

    ラテン語virtus「力・美徳」から。形はなくても力として働いている→「事実上の」→現実に近い働きをする「仮想の」。

    virtue（美徳）と同じ語源。

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    PIE word *wiHrós The adjective is derived from Middle English vertual, virtual [and other forms], from Old French vertüal, vertüelle (modern French virtuel), or from their etymon Medieval Latin virtuālis (“of or pertaining to potency or power; having power to produce an effect, potent; morally virtuous”), from Latin virtūs (“goodness, virtue; manliness, virility”) (from vir (“adult male, man”) (ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *wiHrós (“man”), possibly from *weyh₁- (“to chase, hunt, pursue”)) + -tūs (suffix forming collective or abstract nouns)) + -ālis (suffix forming adjectives of relationship), modelled after virtuōsus (“good, virtuous”).
