    learn

    /lɚn/ 最頻出 頻度 285位

    要点習う、覚える

    意味

    1. 〈知識・技術など,習慣・心構えなど〉を習う,学ぶ,身に着ける
    2. …を覚える(memorize)
    3. …を聞いて知る
    4. 習う,学ぶ,覚える
    5. (…から…について)知る,聞く《+of+名+from+名》

    例文

    • I have to learn many words and phrases by heart.

      私は単語や句をたくさん覚えなければならない。

      Tatoeba #259385

    • No matter what I do, I just can't learn to swim.

      どうやっても泳げるようにならない。

      Tatoeba #10315224

    • He has yet to learn that he has become a father.

      彼は自分が父親になったことをまだ知らない。

      Tatoeba #298552

    語源

    From Middle English lernen (“to learn", also, "to teach"”), from Old English leornian (“to learn", rarely also, "to teach”), from Proto-West Germanic *liʀnōn, from Proto-Germanic *lizaną, from Proto-Indo-European *(le-)lóys-e, stative from the root *leys- (“track, furrow, trace, trail”). The spelling with ⟨ea⟩ is of Early Modern English origin and reflects a lengthening of /ɛ~e/ to /eː/ before /rn/ in Late Old English or Early Middle English, then later lowering to /ɛː/; the modern pronunciation reflects still later reshortening.
