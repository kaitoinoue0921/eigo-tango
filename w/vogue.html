    vogue

    /voʊɡ/ やや難 頻度 10881位

    要点流行[品]

    意味

    1. (ある時期の)(…の)流行[品],はやり[もの]《+for+名》(fashion)

    例文

    • His novel had a great vogue for a long time.

      彼の小説は長い間たいへん人気があった。

      Tatoeba #286736

    • The French style of dress is in vogue now.

      今、フランス風のドレスが流行している。

      Tatoeba #241545

    • Terms like "sexism" are now in vogue.

      「性差別」という言葉が今、流行っている。

      Tatoeba #73622

    語源

    First attested in 1565. Borrowed from Middle French vogue (“wave, course of success”), from Old French vogue, from voguer (“to row, sway, set sail”), from Old Saxon wogōn (“to sway, rock”), var. of wagōn (“to float, fluctuate”), from Proto-Germanic *wagōną (“to sway, fluctuate”) and Proto-Germanic *wēgaz (“water in motion”), from Proto-Germanic *weganą (“to move, carry, weigh”), from Proto-Indo-European *weǵʰ- (“to move, go, transport”) (compare way). Akin to Old Saxon wegan (“to move”), Old High German wegan (“to move”), Old English wegan (“to move, carry, weigh”), Old Norse vaga (“to sway, fluctuate”), Old English wagian (“to sway, totter”), German Woge (“wave”), Swedish våg (“wave”).
