    drab

    /dræb/ やや難 頻度 14529位

    意味

    1. 単調な,退屈な,おもしろくない
    2. くすんだ茶色の
    3. くすんだ茶色,灰色がかったかっ色,土(つち)色

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Probably from Middle French and Old French drap (“cloth”), either: * from Late Latin drappus (“drabcloth, kerchief; piece of cloth”), most likely from Gaulish *drappo, from Proto-Indo-European *drep- (“to scratch, tear”); or * from Frankish *drapi, *drāpi (“that which is fulled, drabcloth”), from Proto-Germanic *drap-, *drēp- (“something beaten”), from *drepaną (“to beat, strike”), from Proto-Indo-European *dʰrebʰ- (“to beat, crush; to make or become thick”).
