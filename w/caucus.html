    caucus

    /kˈɔkəs/ やや難 頻度 11447位

    意味

    1. (政党・団体などの)幹部会議
    2. 幹部会議を開く

    語源

    Unknown. Often claimed to be from an Algonquian language; transcribed words such as cawaassough and caucauasu meaning "counselor, elder, adviser" appear in early texts. A popular folk etymology attested in Great Leaders and National Issues of 1896 stated: "In the early part of the eighteenth century a number of caulkers connected with the shipping business in the North End of Boston held a meeting for consultation.
