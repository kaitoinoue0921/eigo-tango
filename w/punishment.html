    punishment

    /pˈʌnɪʃmənt/ 頻出 頻度 2855位

    意味

    1. 罰を[を受けること]
    2. 処罰,刑罰
    3. ひどい仕打ち,虐待

    例文

    • We won't be able to escape punishment this time.

      今度は罰をまぬがれまい。

      Tatoeba #1224520

    • Many countries have abolished capital punishment.

      多くの国々が処刑を廃止した。

      Tatoeba #274837

    • We won't be able to evade punishment this time.

      今度は罰をまぬがれまい。

      Tatoeba #1224521

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English punishement, from Old French punissement, from punir (“to punish”). Equivalent to punish + -ment. Displaced native Old English wīte.
