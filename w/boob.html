    boob

    /bub/ やや難 頻度 11916位

    意味

    1. まぬけ,ばか
    2. ばかな間違いをする

    例文

    • You boob! I should not have asked you.

      馬鹿たれ！頼まなかったらよかったよ。

      Tatoeba #282400

    語源

    Clipped form of booby (“fool”). Appeared near the beginning of the twentieth century; more information at booby § Etymology 1.
