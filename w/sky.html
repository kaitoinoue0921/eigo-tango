    sky

    /skaɪ/ 最頻出 頻度 792位

    要点空、天国

    意味

    1. 空,上空
    2. 《しばしば複数形で》空模様,天候,気候
    3. 《しばしば複数形で》天国,天
    4. 〈ボール〉を高く打ち上げる

    例文

    • The sky looks ominous. I wonder if it will rain.

      空が怪しい。雨が降るかな。

      Tatoeba #329029

    • As time went on, the sky grew darker and darker.

      時がたつにつれて空は次第に暗くなった。

      Tatoeba #263856

    • The bird went up higher and higher into the sky.

      その鳥は、高く高く空へと昇っていった。

      Tatoeba #45014

    語源

    The noun is derived from Middle English sky (“sky; cloud; mist”), also spelled ski, skie, [and other forms], from Old Norse ský (“cloud”), from Proto-Germanic *skiwją (“cloud; sky”), from *skiwô (“cloud; cloud cover, haze; sky”) (whence Old English sċēo (“cloud”) and Middle English skew (“air; sky; (rare) cloud”)), from Proto-Indo-European *(s)kewH- (“to cover; to conceal, hide”). Partly displaced Old English heofon, which survives in the reflex heaven, still sometimes used in the sense of sky, but usually in high or poetic register. The verb is derived from the noun.
