    adoration

    /ˌædɚˈeɪʃən/ やや難 頻度 11455位

    意味

    1. 崇拝,礼拝
    2. 敬愛,愛慕

    語源

    Borrowed from Middle French adoration, from Latin adōrātiō, adōrātiōnem (“worship, adoration”), from adōrō (“beseech; adore, worship”), from ad (“to, towards”) + ōrō (“beg”). From adore + -ation.
