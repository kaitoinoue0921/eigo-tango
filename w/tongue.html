    tongue

    /tʌŋ/ 頻出 頻度 1463位

    要点舌、言葉、言葉遣い

    意味

    1. 舌
    2. (食用の牛などの)舌,タン
    3. 言葉,言語(language)
    4. 言葉遣い,言い回し
    5. 話す能力
    6. (形・位置・働きが)舌に似たもの
    7. 細長い岬
    8. (靴の)舌革,べろ

    例文

    • My tongue is swollen, and I can't taste a thing!

      舌が腫れて、味覚がない！

      Tatoeba #9846395

    • He cannot speak well that cannot hold his tongue.

      黙ることを知らない者は話し上手ではない。

      Tatoeba #264763

    • Would you mind holding your tongue for a while?

      少し黙っていてもらえませんか。

      Tatoeba #267788

    語源

    From Middle English tongue, a late spelling of tong(e), tung(e), from Old English tunge, from Proto-West Germanic *tungā (“tongue”), from Proto-Germanic *tungǭ (“tongue”), from Proto-Indo-European *dn̥ǵʰwéh₂s (“tongue”). Doublet of langue and lingua. Cognates Germanic: West Frisian tonge, Dutch tong, Luxembourgish Zong, German Zunge, Danish, Norwegian Bokmål, and Norwegian Nynorsk tunge, Faroese, Icelandic, and Swedish tunga, Gothic 𐍄𐌿𐌲𐌲𐍉 (tuggō). Italic: Asturian and Catalan llengua, Aragonese luenga, French langue, Galician, Italian, and Latin lingua, Leonese llingua, Mirandese lhéngua, Portuguese língua, Spanish lengua. Celtic: Irish teanga.
