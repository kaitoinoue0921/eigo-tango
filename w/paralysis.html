    paralysis

    /pɚˈæləsəs/ やや難 頻度 8849位

    意味

    1. (体の)麻痺(まひ),中風
    2. (…の)停滞《+of+名》

    語源

    Borrowed from Latin paralysis, from Ancient Greek παράλυσις (parálusis, “palsy”), from παραλύω (paralúō, “to disable on one side”). By surface analysis, para- + -lysis. Doublet of palsy.
