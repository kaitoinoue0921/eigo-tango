    splendor

    /splˈɛndɚ/ やや難 頻度 12016位

    要点輝き、壮麗

    意味

    1. 輝き,光輝(brilliance)
    2. 壮麗,豪華,壮観
    3. 栄光,栄誉

    語源

    From Anglo-Norman splendur, splendour, or directly from its source Latin splendor, from the verb splendeō (“to shine”).
