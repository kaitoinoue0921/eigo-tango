    risk（リスク）

    /rɪsk/ 頻出 頻度 1102位 英検2級

    英検2級危険にさらす・危険

    意味

    1. 危険;危害(損害)を受ける可能性《+of+名(doing)》
    2. 〈生命など〉を危険にさらす
    3. …を危険(失敗,損失など)を覚悟してする

    例文

    • He saved his friend at the risk of his own life.

      彼は自分自身の命をかけて友人を救った。

      Tatoeba #298901

    • Chris risks falling behind in the science class.

      クリスは理科の授業で落ちこぼれそうです。

      Tatoeba #3566698

    • Is there any risk of being caught by the police?

      警察に捕まるような恐れがあるのですか。

      Tatoeba #238193

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From earlier risque, from Middle French risque, from Old Italian risco (“risk”) (modern Italian rischio) and rischiare (“to run into danger”). Displaced native Old English pleoh (“risk”) and plēon (“to risk”). Speculation on earlier roots Most dictionaries consider the etymology of these Italian terms uncertain, but some suggest they perhaps come from Vulgar Latin *resecum (“that which cuts, rock, crag”) (> Medieval Latin resicu), from Latin resecō (“cut off, loose, curtail”, verb), in the sense of that which is a danger to boating or shipping; or from Ancient Greek ῥιζικόν (rhizikón, “root, radical, hazard”). A few dictionaries express more certainty.
