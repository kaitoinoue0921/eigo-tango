    digger

    /dˈɪɡɚ/ やや難 頻度 11674位

    意味

    1. 掘る人,坑夫
    2. 採掘器具

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English dyggar, equivalent to dig + -er. In the sense of "Australian soldier", attributed to the considerable time that soldiers spent digging trenches during World War I.
