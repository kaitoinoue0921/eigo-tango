    mulatto

    /məlˈɑtoʊ/ やや難 頻度 13924位

    意味

    1. 白人と黒人の混血児

    語源

    Borrowed from Portuguese and/or Spanish mulato (“of mixed breed, young mule”), from mulo (“mule”), from Latin mūlus (“mule”). Perhaps an allusion to the hybrid origin of mules. Doublet of mulato.
