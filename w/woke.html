    woke

    /woʊk/ 最頻出 頻度 970位

    意味

    1. wake の過去

    例文

    • What were you dreaming about when I woke you up?

      僕が起こしたとき、どんな夢を見ていたの？

      Tatoeba #3666109

    • When I woke up this morning, it was raining hard.

      朝起きたら、強い雨が降ってたんだ。

      Tatoeba #10017226

    • He woke up to find himself lying on a park bench.

      目を覚ますと、彼は公園のベンチに横になっていた。

      Tatoeba #323779

    語源

    Shortened from woken or woken up, or derived from dialectal use of woke (past participle of wake). The sense of being aware of social injustice dates to at least the 1930s. The derogatory use has been most commonly used as of the 2020s.
