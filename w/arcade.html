    arcade

    /ɑrkˈeɪd/ やや難 頻度 7556位

    意味

    1. 拱廊(きょうろう)(建物の側面につけた廊下状の通路)
    2. アーケード(両側に商店が並ぶ屋根つき街路)

    例文

    • The shopping arcade was covered with lots of paper decorations.

      アーケード天井にたくさんの張りぼてが飾られてた。

      Tatoeba #326796

    語源

    Etymology tree Proto-Indo-European *h₂erkʷ- Proto-Indo-European *h₂erkʷos Proto-Italic *arkuos Latin arcus Italian arcatabor. French arcadeubor. English arcade Unadapted borrowing from French arcade, from Italian arcata (“the arch of a bridge”), from Latin arcus (“an arc”). Not etymologically related to Arcadia.
