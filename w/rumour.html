    rumour

    /rˈumɚ/ 標準 頻度 4966位

    要点うわさ,

    意味

    1. うわさ,うわさ話,流言
    2. =rumor

    例文

    • Certainty, it was an unsubstantiated rumour.

      もちろん、デマだったよ。

      Tatoeba #13694567

    • It's not fiction or rumour, etc., it's a true story.

      作り話やうわさなどでなく、本当にあった話です。

      Tatoeba #7919867

    • Obviously, it was an unsubstantiated rumour.

      もちろん、デマだったよ。

      Tatoeba #13694570

    語源

    From Middle English rumour, from Old French rumour, rumor, from Latin rūmor (“common talk”), ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *rewH- (“to shout, roar”).
