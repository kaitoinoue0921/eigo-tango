    succeed

    /səksˈid/ 頻出 頻度 1368位

    要点成功する、継承する、後任となる、続く

    意味

    1. 〈計画などが〉成功する,うまくいく
    2. 〈人が〉(…に)成功する《+in+名(doing)》
    3. 〈人が〉(…を)継承する,相続する《+to+名》
    4. 〈物事が〉続く,後に来る
    5. 〈人〉の後任となる
    6. …に続く

    例文

    • No doubt he did his best, but he didn't succeed.

      確かに彼は最善を尽くしたが、成功しなかった。

      Tatoeba #21695

    • A man as diligent as Tom is, is sure to succeed.

      トムのような勤勉な人は、絶対成功するって。

      Tatoeba #10118239

    • I tried again and again, but I couldn't succeed.

      何度もやってみたが、うまくいかなかった。

      Tatoeba #24487

    語源

    From Old French succeder, from Latin succedere (“to go under, go from under, come under, approach, follow, take the place of, receive by succession, prosper, be successful”). Partly displaced English speed, of which the original sense “to succeed” only survives in a few fossilized expressions such as Godspeed.
