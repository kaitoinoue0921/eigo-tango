    kelp

    /kɛlp/ やや難 頻度 10807位

    意味

    1. ケルプ(コンブ・アラメなど大形の海草の総称)
    2. ケルプ灰(ヨードを含む)

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Inherited from Middle English *kelpe, culpe, *kilpe (Early Modern English kilpe), of unclear origin, though the attested forms point to Old English *cylp, possibly from Proto-West Germanic *kulpi, from Proto-Germanic *kulpiz (“lump, boll”, referencing the airsacks of species such as bladderwrack and knotweed), potentially a substrate term connected to Latin globus (“globe”) and Proto-Germanic *klumpô (“clump”).
