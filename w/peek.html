    peek

    /pik/ やや難 頻度 6639位

    意味

    1. (…を)こっそりちらっとのぞく《+at(in,into)+名》
    2. 《単数形で》ちらっとのぞき見ること,のぞき見

    例文

    • You can see the sun peeking through the clouds.

      雲の切れ間から太陽が見えます。

      Tatoeba #26517

    • Tom opened the box and peeked inside.

      トムは箱を開け、中をのぞき込んだ。

      Tatoeba #2054026

    • You should stop peeking when people are using their cellphones.

      人が携帯いじってるときに覗き込む癖やめた方がいいよ。

      Tatoeba #3182882

    語源

    From Middle English *peken, piken, pyken (“to peep”), probably a fusion of Middle English pepen (“to peep”) and keken, kiken (“to keek, look, spy”), equivalent to a blend of peep + keek. Perhaps also possibly a metathetic alteration of Middle English kepen, kipen, kypen (“to keep, look, observe, watch”).
