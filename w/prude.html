    prude

    /prud/ やや難 頻度 10240位

    意味

    1. (言動・服装などで)お上品ぶった人,すまし屋

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From French prude, from Old French prude, prode, feminine of prou, prod, prud (“good, excellent, brave”), from Latin prōde. Related to proud but unrelated to prudent.
