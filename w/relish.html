    relish

    /rˈɛlɪʃ/ やや難 頻度 12612位 英検準1級

    英検準1級楽しむ・好む・味わい

    意味

    1. (食物の独特の)風味
    2. 調味料,薬味
    3. 《通例否定文で》(…に対する)興味,好み《+for+名》
    4. 《時にa~》おもしろみ,興趣
    5. 〈物・事〉を楽しむ,好む
    6. (…の)味がする,風味がある《+of+名》

    例文

    • He did not relish this simple family life.

      彼にはこの単調な家庭生活がおもしろくなかった。

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    The noun is a variant of release (“(obsolete) odour, scent”), from Middle English reles, relese (“odour, scent; taste; efficacy, power”), probably from Anglo-Norman reles, relais, or Old French reles, relais (“that which is left behind, remainder, residue”), from relaisser, relaschier (“to liberate, release; to relax”) (modern French relâcher), from Latin relaxāre, the present active infinitive of relaxō (“to stretch out or widen again, loosen, slacken; (figurative) to ease, relax”), from re- (prefix meaning ‘again; back, backwards’) + laxō (“to release, undo; to relax”) (from laxus (“spacious, wide; loose, slack”) (ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *(s)leg- (“to slacken; to tire out”)) +…
