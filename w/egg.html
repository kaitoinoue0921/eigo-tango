    egg

    /ɛɡ/ 頻出 頻度 1801位

    要点卵

    意味

    1. 卵,卵細胞
    2. 鳥の卵;(特に)鶏卵
    3. 《英話》《形容詞を伴って》(…な)やつ
    4. 《俗》爆弾,手投げ弾
    5. 〈人〉を扇動(激励)する;〈人〉をそそのかして…させる《+名+on to do》

    例文

    • When Tom was young, he ate three eggs every day.

      トムは若かりし頃、卵を毎日３つ食べてました。

      Tatoeba #1023757

    • You cannot make an omelet without breaking eggs.

      卵を割らなくてはオムレツは作れない。

      Tatoeba #325240

    • The reason is that I need one container of eggs.

      １パック卵がいるんだ。

      Tatoeba #10207551

    語源

    The noun is derived from Middle English eg, egg, egge (“egg of a domestic or wild fowl; egg of a snake”) [and other forms] (originally Northern England and Northeast Midlands), from Old Norse egg (“egg”), from Proto-Germanic *ajją (“egg”) (by Holtzmann’s law), from Proto-Indo-European *h₂ōwyóm (“egg”), probably from *h₂éwis (“bird”), from *h₂ew- (“to consume”). Doublet of huevo, oeuf, and ovum.
