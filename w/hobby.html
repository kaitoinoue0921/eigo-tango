    hobby

    /hˈɑbi/ 頻出 頻度 2296位

    要点趣味

    意味

    1. (他の人と多少変わった)趣味,道楽

    例文

      私の趣味のひとつは造花をつくることです。

    • My hobbies are traveling and watching baseball.

      趣味は旅行と野球観戦です。

      私の趣味は、魚釣りとテレビを見る事です。

      Tatoeba #19339

    語源

    From Middle English hoby (“small horse, pony”), a variant or alteration of hobyn (“a small ambling horse”), of uncertain and disputed origin. According to one theory the term is derived from Hobin, a pet name of Robert or Robin, at the time frequently used as a proper name for a workhorse (compare dobbin).
