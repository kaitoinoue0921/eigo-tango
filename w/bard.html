    bard

    /bɑrd/ やや難 頻度 13259位

    意味

    1. ケルト族の吟遊詩人
    2. (一般に)詩人

    例文

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Scottish Gaelic bàrd, from Old Irish bard, from Proto-Celtic *bardos (“bard, poet”), from Proto-Indo-European *gʷerH- (“praise”), and reinforced by Latin bardus, borrowed from Celtic. Cognate with Latin grātus (“grateful, pleasant, delightful”), Sanskrit गृणाति (gṛṇāti, “calls, praises”), Old Church Slavonic жрьти (žrĭti, “to sacrifice”).
