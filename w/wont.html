    wont

    /woʊnt/ やや難 頻度 9263位

    意味

    1. 《be wont to do》…し慣れて,するのを常として
    2. (特定の人の)習慣

    例文

    • We were wont to meet at that pleasant spot.

      われわれはあの楽しい場所で会うのが常だった。

      Tatoeba #28859

    • It was my father's wont to read the newspaper before breakfast.

      朝食前に新聞を読むのが父の習慣だった。

      Tatoeba #277818

    語源

    From Middle English wont, from Old English ġewunod, past participle of ġewunian (“to be accustomed to, dwell”), the noun sense possibly arising via conflation of wone (“custom, habit, practice”) and the adjective wont. Compare German Low German Gewohnte (“custom, habit”), Dutch gewoonte, Swedish van. Likely related to wone, wonder, wean, and win.
