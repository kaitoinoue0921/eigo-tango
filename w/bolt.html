    bolt

    /boʊlt/ 標準 頻度 4782位

    要点ボルト、かんぬき、いなずま

    意味

    1. ボルト(木・鉄板を締め合わせる締めくぎ)
    2. (戸締まりの)かんぬき,差し錠
    3. 石弓(crossbow)用の短く太い矢
    4. いなずま,電光
    5. 《単数形で》(突然の)脱走,逃亡
    6. (布・紙の)一巻き《+of+名》
    7. 《米》脱党
    8. 〈戸・窓など〉にかんぬきを掛ける;…をかんぬきを掛けて閉じ込める
    9. 《米》…と関係を絶つ
    10. 〈食べ物〉をうのみにする,大急ぎで食べる
    11. 急に飛び出す,急に駆け出す
    12. まっすぐに
    13. …をふるい分ける

    例文

    • It came to the president like a bolt of lightning.

      社長のとっさのひらめきなんだって。

      Tatoeba #265370

    • The news of her death came as a bolt from the blue.

      彼女の死の知らせはまさに青天の霹靂だった。

      Tatoeba #309331

    • My father taught me the nuts and bolts of gardening.

      父は園芸の基本を教えてくれた。

      Tatoeba #319080

    Etymology tree Proto-Indo-European *bʰeld- Proto-Indo-European *-os Proto-Indo-European *bʰóldos? Proto-Germanic *bultaz Proto-West Germanic *bolt Old English bolt Middle English bolt English bolt From Middle English bolt, from Old English bolt, from Proto-West Germanic *bolt, from Proto-Germanic *bultaz, perhaps from Proto-Indo-European *bʰeld- (“to knock, strike”). Compare Lithuanian beldu (“I knock”), baldas (“pole for striking”). Akin to Dutch and West Frisian bout, German Bolz or Bolzen, Danish bolt, Swedish bult, Icelandic bolti. The association of thunder and lightning with 'bolts' is found back into prehistory in many cultures, at least in Eurasia.
