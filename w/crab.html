    crab

    /kræb/ やや難 頻度 6062位

    意味

    1. カニ;カニの肉
    2. 《話》気むずかし屋
      ぶつぶつ不平を言う
      …をふくれさせる
      = crab louse(毛ジラミ)

    例文

    • Shrimps, crabs, and lobsters are crustaceans.

      エビ・カニ・ロブスターは甲殻類です。

      Tatoeba #10209455

    • The red king crab is a large crustacean.

      鱈場蟹は大型甲殻類である。

      Tatoeba #837353

    • You cannot make a crab walk straight.

      蟹を縦に歩かせることはできない。

      Tatoeba #22063

    語源

    From Middle English crabbe, from Old English crabba (“crab; crayfish; cancer”), from Proto-West Germanic *krabbō, from Proto-Germanic *krabbô, from *krabbōną (“to scratch”), from Proto-Indo-European *grobʰeh₂yéti (“to scratch”), a metathesised o-grade of *gerbʰ- (“to carve, scratch”). More at carve. Cognates See also Dutch krab, Low German Krabb, Danish krabbe, Swedish krabba. Further cognates with frequentative-infix are Saterland Frisian krabbelje (“to creep, crawl”), Dutch krabbelen (“to scratch”) and German krabbeln (“to crawl”). Possibly related to English creep and Swedish krypa (“to creep, crawl”) etc.
