    wrestle

    /rˈɛsəl/ やや難 頻度 7678位

    要点格闘する、取り組む

    意味

    1. (…と)格闘する,レスリングをする《+with+名》
    2. (問題などと)取り組む;(疑念・良心などと)戦う《+with+名》
    3. …とレスリングをする,格闘する
    4. レスリング,格闘;苦痛

    例文

    • He wrestled his attacker to the ground.

      彼は襲撃者を格闘して地面に倒した。

      Tatoeba #299309

    • Do you want to arm wrestle?

      腕相撲しようか？

      Tatoeba #2793707

    語源

    The verb is derived from Middle English wrestlen, wrastlen (“to engage in grappling combat or sport, struggle, wrestle; to twist and turn, squirm, wriggle, writhe; (figurative) to contend, grapple with, struggle”), from Old English wrǣstlian (“to wrestle”), a frequentative form of wrǣstan (“to twist, wrest”), from Proto-Germanic *wraistijaną (“to turn; to twist, wrest”), ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *wreyt- (“to twist”). By surface analysis, wrest + -le (frequentative suffix). Probably related to wraxle (UK, dialectal, archaic). The noun is derived from the verb.
