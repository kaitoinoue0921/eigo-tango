    deli

    /dˈɛli/ やや難 頻度 12861位

    意味

    1. デリ(惣菜店)《略》/ 惣菜
    2. =delicatessen

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Attested since 1954. Clipping of delicatessen, from German Delikatessen, plural of Delikatesse (“delicacy, fine food”), from French délicatesse, derived from the adjective délicat, from Latin delicatus.
