    innermost

    /ˈɪnɚmˌoʊst/ やや難 頻度 11976位

    意味

    1. いちばん奥の,最も深い,心の奥底の

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English innermost, innermest, in-nermast, alteration (due to Middle English inner, innere (“inner”)) of Old English innemest (“innermost”), equivalent to inner + -most.
