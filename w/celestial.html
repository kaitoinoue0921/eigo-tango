    celestial

    /səlˈɛstʃəl/ やや難 頻度 6343位 TOEFL

    TOEFL天体の・天の

    意味

    1. 天国の,天上界の;神聖な;絶妙な
    2. 天体の,天空の

    例文

    • I will become god of the celestial world.

      僕は神世界の神になる。

      Tatoeba #6476005

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    The adjective is derived from Late Middle English celestial (“relating to the heavens or sky; (Christianity) relating to heaven, divine, heavenly”), borrowed from Old French celestial (modern French céleste), from Medieval Latin caelestiālis (“celestial”), or directly from its etymon Latin caelestis (“of or in the heavens, heavenly; (figurative) of the gods, divine; etc.”), from caelum (“heaven; sky”) (ultimate etymology uncertain, possibly from Proto-Indo-European *kéh₂ilom (“whole”)) + -estris (suffix meaning ‘dwelling or located in’ forming adjectives from nouns). The adverb and noun are derived from the adjective.
