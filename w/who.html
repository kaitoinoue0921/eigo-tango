    who

    /hu/ 最頻出 頻度 71位

    要点だれが、ところの、そしてその人は(を)

    意味

    1. 世界保健機関(World Health Organization)
    2. 《疑問代名詞》だれが,どんな人が
    3. 《関係代名詞》(先行詞は「人」)
    4. 《制限用法》(…する,…した)ところの
    5. 《非制限用法》そしてその人は(を),それは…する(した)人だが
    6. 《古》《先行詞を含んで》…する人[はだれでも]

    例文

    • Who was it that bought those pictures yesterday?

      昨日その絵を買ったのは誰だろう。

      Tatoeba #244394

    • He who touches pitch shall be defiled therewith.

      朱に交われば赤くなる。

      Tatoeba #266076

    • Who has been absent from school for three weeks?

      ３週間学校を休んでいる人はだれですか。

      Tatoeba #72687

    語源

    From Middle English who, hwo, huo, wha, hwoa, hwa, from Old English hwā (dative hwām, genitive hwæs), from Proto-West Germanic *hwaʀ, from Proto-Germanic *hwaz, from Proto-Indo-European *kʷos, *kʷís. The sound change /hw/ > /h/ (without a corresponding change in spelling) was due to wh-cluster reduction after an irregular change of /ɑː/ to /oː/ in Middle English (instead of the expected /ɔː/) and further to /uː/ regularly in Early Modern English. A similar change occurred in two. Compare how, which underwent wh-reduction earlier (in Old English), and thus is spelt with h.
