    infantile

    /ˈɪnfəntɪl/ やや難 頻度 13797位

    意味

    1. 《名詞の前にのみ用いて》幼時の
    2. 子供じみた;子供らしい

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Mid-15th century, "pertaining to infants," from Latin infantilis (“pertaining to an infant”), equivalent to infant + -ile, from īnfāns. Sense of "infant-like" is from 1772.
