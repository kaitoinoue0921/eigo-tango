    penal

    /pˈinəl/ やや難 頻度 13798位

    意味

    1. (特に,法的な)刑罰の
    2. 刑事上の

    語源

    From Old French peinal, from Medieval Latin penalis, from Latin poenalis, from poena (“punishment”), from Ancient Greek ποινή (poinḗ, “punishment”), from Proto-Indo-European *kʷoynéh₂.
