    agility

    /ədʒˈɪləti/ やや難 頻度 11093位

    意味

    1. (動作または頭の働きの)機敏さ,明敏さ

    語源

    From late Middle English, borrowed from Middle French agilité, from Latin agilitās, from agilis (“nimble, fleet, quick”), equivalent to agile + -ity.
