    purpose

    /pˈɚpəs/ 頻出 頻度 1163位

    要点目的、用途

    意味

    1. (人の抱く)目的,目標,意図
    2. (物事の)用途,目的,効果
    3. (目的を達成しようとする)決意,意志
    4. …を目的とする,もくろむ

    例文

    • This plan will serve its purpose to some extent.

      この計画はある程度の効果が見込める。

      Tatoeba #59532

    • It is likely that he kept me waiting on purpose.

      彼は私をわざと待たせたようだ。

      Tatoeba #298091

    • I don't understand the purpose of your question.

      質問の意図が分かりません。

      Tatoeba #2582897

    語源

    From Middle English purpos, from Old French purposer (“to propose”) (with conjugation altered based on poser), from Latin prō- (“forth”) + pōnere (“place, put”), hence Latin prōpōnō, prōpōnere. By surface analysis, pur- + pose (“halt, pause, put, place”).
