    excel

    /ɪksˈɛl/ やや難 頻度 8148位

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English excellen, from Old French exceller, from Latin excellere, excelsum; ex (“out”) + *cellō, an unattested verb root found in culmen (“height, top”); Compare French exceller. See also culminate, column.
