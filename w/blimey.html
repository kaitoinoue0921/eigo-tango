    blimey

    やや難 頻度 10480位

    意味

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    A minced oath derived from (God) blind me (either concurrent with or from a clipping of cor blimey or gorblimey), or blame me. Compare crivvens, drat, etc.
