    pamphlet

    /pˈæmflət/ やや難 頻度 8125位

    要点パンフレット

    意味

    1. パンフレット,小冊子;(特に時事問題の)小論文

    例文

    • The breakfast menu has been written in the pamphlet.

      朝食のメニューはパンフレットに書いてあります。

      Tatoeba #11162153

    • Please look at the pamphlet for the details.

      詳しくはパンフレットをご覧ください。

      Tatoeba #2678426

    • Please help me distribute these pamphlets to visitors.

      来てくれた人にこのパンフレットを配るのを手伝って下さい。

      Tatoeba #324994

    語源

    The noun is derived from Late Middle English pamflet, pamphilet (“short written text; small book; tract”) [and other forms], from Middle French Pamphilet (compare Late Latin (Anglo-Latin) pamfletus, panfletus, paunflettus (“short written text”), Old French Panfilès), a popular shorthand for the 12th-century Latin love poem Pamphilus, seu de amore (Pamphilus, or On Love): the widely circulated pamphlets then gave this name to the whole phenomenon.
