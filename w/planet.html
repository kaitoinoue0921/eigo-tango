    planet（プラネット）

    /plˈænət/ 頻出 頻度 1054位

    要点惑星

    意味

    1. 惑星,遊星

    例文

    • Neptune is the eighth planet of the solar system.

      海王星は太陽系の8番目の惑星だ。

      Tatoeba #22212

    • Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System.

      木星は、太陽系最大の惑星である。

      Tatoeba #499549

    • Do you think that Earth is a beautiful planet?

      地球って、きれいな星だと思う？

      Tatoeba #10617637

    語源

    ギリシャ語planetes「さまよう者」から。星座の中をさまようように動いて見える星→「惑星」。

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English planete, from Old French planete, from Latin planeta, planetes, from Ancient Greek πλανήτης (planḗtēs, “wanderer”) (itself an ellipsis of ἀστέρες πλανῆται (astéres planêtai, “wandering stars”)), from Ancient Greek πλανάω (planáō, “wander about, stray”), of unknown origin. Cognate with Latin pālor (“wander about, stray”), Old Norse flana (“to rush about”), and Norwegian flanta (“to wander about”). More at flaunt. So called because they have apparent motion, unlike the "fixed" stars. Originally including also the moon and sun but not the Earth; modern scientific sense of "world that orbits a star" is from 1630s in English.
