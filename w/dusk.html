    dusk

    /dʌsk/ 標準 頻度 5606位

    意味

    1. (特に夕暮れの)たそがれ
    2. (ある所の)薄暗がり

    例文

    • I'm beat. I've been working from dawn to dusk.

      もう朝から晩まで働いてクタクタだよ。

      Tatoeba #31189

    • At dusk, the sky is tinted with purple light.

      夕暮れ時、空は紫色の光に染まっています。

      Tatoeba #13557590

    • When it's dusk in Germany, it's late at night in Japan.

      ドイツの黄昏時は日本の深夜です。

      Tatoeba #10044751

    語源

    From Middle English dosk, dusk(e) (“dusky”, adj.), from Old English dox (“dark, swarthy”), from Proto-Germanic *duskaz (“dark, smoky”), from Proto-Indo-European *dʰwes-, related to *dʰewh₂- (“smoke, mist, haze”). Cognate to Latin fuscus (“dark, dusky”), Sanskrit धूसर (dhūsara, “dust-colored”), Old Irish donn (“dark”). Related to dye, dust and dun (see these for more). Doublet of fuscous.
