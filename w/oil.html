    oil

    /ɔɪl/ 最頻出 頻度 867位

    要点油

    意味

    1. 油;油状物
    2. =petroleum
    3. 《複数形で》=oil paint
    4. =oil painting
    5. …に油を塗る,油を引く,油を差す

      日本は石油を外国に依存している。

      キャンバスのオイルはとても微細な花びらを描けない。

    語源

    Etymology tree Proto-Hellenic *élaiwon Ancient Greek ἔλαιον (élaion)der. Latin oleumder. Latin olea Old French oilebor. Middle English oyle English oil From Middle English oyle, oile (“olive oil”), borrowed from Old French oile, from Latin oleum (“oil, olive oil”), from Ancient Greek ἔλαιον (élaion, “olive oil”), from ἐλαία (elaía, “olive”). Compare Proto-Slavic *lojь. More at olive. Doublet of oleum. Supplanted Middle English ele (“oil”), from Old English ele (“oil”), also from Latin.
