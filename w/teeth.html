    teeth

    /tiθ/ 頻出 頻度 1004位

    意味

    1. tooth の複数形

    例文

    • Because my teeth hurt, I couldn't open my mouth.

      歯が痛くて、口が開けれないの。

      Tatoeba #12622760

    • If we care for our teeth we won't have cavities.

      歯をきちんと手入れしていれば虫歯にはならない。

      Tatoeba #263593

    • No matter how sleepy you are, brush your teeth.

      どんなに眠くても、歯は磨きなさい。

      Tatoeba #1475472

    語源

    From Middle English teth, plural of tothe, from Old English tēþ, nominative plural of tōþ, from earlier *tœ̄þ, from Proto-Germanic *tanþiz, nominative plural of *tanþs, from Proto-Indo-European *h₃dóntes, nominative plural of *h₃dónts.
