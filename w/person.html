    person

    /pˈɚsən/ 最頻出 頻度 238位

    要点人、体、容姿

    意味

    1. (性別・年齢に関係なく)人
    2. (人間の)体,身体
    3. 《単数形で》容姿,身なり
    4. (文法で)人称

    例文

    • This is the person I talked about the other day.

      こちらは先日私がお話しした人です。

      Tatoeba #1108056

    • This person always plays the role of a criminal.

      この人いつも犯人役だな。

      Tatoeba #13706636

    • A small boy needs some person he can look up to.

      子供にはだれか尊敬できる人が必要です。

      Tatoeba #245817

    語源

    Etymology tree Latin persōna Anglo-Norman persouneder. Middle English persoun English person From Middle English persoun, personne et al., from Anglo-Norman parsone, persoun et al. (Old French persone (“human being”), French personne), and its source Latin persōna (“mask used by actor; role, part, character”), perhaps a loanword from Etruscan 𐌘𐌄𐌓𐌔𐌖 (φersu, “mask”). In this sense, displaced native man, which came to mean primarily "adult male" in Middle English; see Old English mann. Displaced native Old English hād (“person, individual”). Doublet of parson and persona.
