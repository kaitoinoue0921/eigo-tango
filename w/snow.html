    snow

    /snoʊ/ 最頻出 頻度 615位

    要点雪、降雪、雪が降る

    意味

    1. 雪
    2. 《単数形で》降雪;《複数形で》積雪
    3. 雪のような物;(特に)コカイン,ヘロイン
    4. 《itを主語にして》雪が降る
    5. (…に)雪のように降る《+on+名》;雪のように舞い込む《+in》
    6. …を雪のように降らせる
    7. …を雪でおおう(閉じ込める)《+名+in(under, up)》

    例文

    • The snow prevented the airplane from taking off.

      雪のため飛行機は離陸出来なかった。

      Tatoeba #272577

    • It won't be long before the snow starts to thaw.

      雪解けも間近です。

      Tatoeba #10317988

    • We have snow on the mountain all the year round.

      その山は一年中雪をいただいている。

      Tatoeba #47717

    語源

    The noun is derived from Middle English snaw, snou, snow (“snow; accumulation of snow; snowfall; snowstorm; whiteness”), from Old English snāw (“snow”), from Proto-West Germanic *snaiw (“snow”), from Proto-Germanic *snaiwaz (“snow”), from Proto-Indo-European *snóygʷʰos (“snow”), from *sneygʷʰ- (“to snow”). The verb is derived from Middle English snouen (“to snow; (figurative) to shower”), from snou, snow (noun) (see above) + -en (suffix forming the infinitive of verbs). Displaced Old English snīwan, whence English snew (obsolete). Verb etymology 1, verb sense 2.3.2 (“to convince or hoodwink (someone)”) probably refers to a person being blinded or confused by a snowstorm.
