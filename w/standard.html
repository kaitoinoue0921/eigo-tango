    意味

    1. 標準,基準,水準,規範
    2. (度量衡の)基本単位
    3. (貨幣制度の)本位
    4. 旗,軍旗;(主義・主張などの)旗じるし
    5. 支柱,ランプ台,しょく台
    6. 標準の,標準的な
    7. 定評のある,権威のある

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English standard, from Old French estandart (“gathering place, battle flag”), from Frankish *standahard (literally “stand firm, stand hard”), equivalent to stand + -ard. An alternative etymology derives the second element from Frankish *oʀd (“point, spot, place”) (compare Old French ordé (“pointed”), Old English ord (“point, source, vanguard”), German Standort (“location, place, site, position, base”, literally “standing-point”)). Merged with Middle English standar, stander, standere (“flag, banner”, literally “stander”), equivalent to stand + -er. More at stand, hard, ord.
