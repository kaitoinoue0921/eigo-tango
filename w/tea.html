    teaティー

    /ti/ 最頻出 頻度 593位

    要点茶、午後のお茶、お茶の会

    意味

    1. 茶,(特に)紅茶
    2. (乾燥した)茶の葉;茶の木
    3. 茶に似た飲料,煎じ茶
    4. 午後のお茶
    5. お茶の会(午後の軽食を出すパーティー)

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    First appears c. 1655, in the English translation Álvaro Semedo’s writings. From Dutch thee, from Hokkien 茶 (tê) (Amoy dialect), from Old Chinese, ultimately from Proto-Sino-Tibetan *s-la (“leaf, tea”). Introduced to English and other Western European languages by the Dutch East India Company, who sourced their tea in Amoy; compare Malay teh along the same trade route. Doublet of chai and cha (and, distantly, the first element of lahpet), from same Proto-Sino-Tibetan root; see discussion of cognates. Cognates The word for “tea” in many languages is of Sinitic origin (due to China being the origin of the plant), and thus there are many cognates; see translations.
