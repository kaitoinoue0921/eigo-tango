    forest（フォレスト）

    /fˈɔrəst/ 最頻出 頻度 845位

    要点森林

    意味

    1. (広い地域にわたる)森林,山林
    2. 《通例単数形で》(…の)林《+of+名》

    例文

    • Because of these trees, he can't see the forest.

      この木のせいで森を見ることができません。

      Tatoeba #766875

    • Water shortage is caused by the loss of forests.

      水不足は森林の喪失によって起こる。

      Tatoeba #270928

    • There is a dense growth of vines in this forest.

      この森には野ブドウが密集して生えている。

      Tatoeba #58340

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Inherited from Middle English forest, from Old French forest, from Early Medieval Latin forestis. The Latin could be: * from foris (“outside”), as in forestis (silva) "(wood) outside," * or from Frankish or Proto-West Germanic *furhisti (“forest, fir-grove, wooded land”), equivalent to fir + hurst. In which case, related to Old English fyrhþe (“forested land”), Old High German forst, forsti (“forest”), Old Norse fýri (“pine forest”). Doublet of frith. Cognate with Dutch vorst (“copse, grove, woodland”), German Forst (“forest”).
