    hooky

    /hˈʊki/ やや難 頻度 10305位

    意味

    1. 精液, 愛液;まずい[まずそうな]食い物
    2. かぎ(フック)状の
    3. くすねる

    例文

    • Every school kid has played hooky at one time or another.

      多くの学童は、少なくとも一回以上、ズル休みをしたことがあるだろう。

      Tatoeba #1188661

    • Tom played hooky from school.

      トムは、学校をズル休みした。

      Tatoeba #10559435

    • Did you play hooky yesterday?

      昨日学校をさぼったの？

      Tatoeba #7849034

    語源

    First attested in 1848 in New York City. Most likely from Dutch hoekje (“nook, corner; 'spot to hide' in hide-and-seek”). Formerly, "hoekje spelen" could be used to mean "to play hide-and-seek", though the common term for the game nowadays is verstoppertje. By surface analysis, hook + -y.
