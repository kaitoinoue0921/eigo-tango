    butt

    /bʌt/ 標準 頻度 3118位

    意味

    1. …を頭(角)で突く(押す)
    2. 頭(角)で突く(押す)
    3. 頭(角)で突くこと(押すこと),頭突き
    4. あざけり(批評などの)的;笑いもの
    5. (武器・道具の)太いほうの端
    6. 残片
    7. (ぶどう酒などの)大だる,一たる

    例文

    • The cause of the fire was his cigarette butt.

      火事の原因は彼のタバコの火の不始末だ。

      Tatoeba #23825

    • Lois always butts into our conversation.

      ロイはいつも僕たちの話に口をはさむんだ。

      Tatoeba #29476

    • Sheesh, you've even got cigarette butts scattered around.

      もう・・・タバコの吸殻まで散らばってるし。

      Tatoeba #328339

    語源

    From Middle English but, butte (“goal, mark, butt of land”), from Old English byt, bytt (“small piece of land”) and *butt (attested in diminutive Old English buttuc (“end, small piece of land”) > English buttock), from Proto-West Germanic *butt, from Proto-Germanic *buttaz (“end, piece”), from Proto-Indo-European *bʰudʰnós (“bottom”), later thematic variant of Proto-Indo-European *bʰudʰmḗn ~ *bʰudʰn-, perhaps from Proto-Indo-European *dʰewbʰ- (“deep”). Cognate with Norwegian butt (“stump, block”), Faroese buttur (“wooden block”), Icelandic bútur (“piece, fragment”), Low German butt (“blunt, clumsy”).
