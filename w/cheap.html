    cheap

    /tʃip/ 頻出 頻度 1435位

    要点値段が安い、安っぽい

    意味

    1. 値段が安い,料金が安い,安く売る
    2. 安っぽい,安物の,質の悪い,価値のない
    3. 下品な,野卑な
    4. 簡単に手に入る
    5. 《おもに米》(人が)けちな,けちくさい(stingy)
    6. 安く
    7. 安っぽく

    例文

    • I've never seen instant ramen this cheap before.

      こんな安い袋ラーメン、初めて見た。

      Tatoeba #13231566

    • This chair was cheap, but it's very comfortable.

      この椅子は安かったけど、すごい快適よ。

      Tatoeba #12415656

    • This ring is expensive. Show me some cheap ones.

      この指輪は高い。もっと安いのを見せてください。

      Tatoeba #59068

    語源

    古英語ceap「取り引き」から。good cheap「よい買い物」が縮まり、得な値段→「安い」。

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    As a noun, from Middle English chep, from Old English cēap (“trade, market, value”), from Proto-West Germanic *kaup. As a verb, from Middle English chepen, from Old English ċēapian (“to buy, bargain, trade”), from Proto-West Germanic *kaupōn, from Proto-Germanic *kaupōną, a verbal derivative of *kaupô (“trader”), from Latin caupō. The adjective originated as a shortening of Middle and Early Modern English good cheap, literally “good purchase” (as in “that was good cheap”, i.e. “that was [a] good purchase”). Compare Dutch goedkoop, French bon marché.
