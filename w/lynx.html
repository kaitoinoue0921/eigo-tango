    lynx

    /lɪŋks/ やや難 頻度 11715位

    意味

    1. オオヤマネコ(ヤマネコの一種;目が鋭く耳の先に房状の毛がある)

    例文

    • It can take prey by force from other predators, such as wolves and lynx.

      他の捕食動物(オオカミ、ヤマネコ)の獲物を力ずくで奪うことができる。

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English lynx, linx, lenx, lynce, from Latin lynx, from Ancient Greek λύγξ (lúnx), from Proto-Hellenic *lúnks, from Proto-Indo-European *lewk- (“white; light; bright”), because of the cat's seemingly glowing eyes and ability to see in the dark. Doublet of onza and ounce. Displaced English los; Middle English lusk (“lynx”), from Old English lox (“lynx”) as the animal died out in Britain during the Middle Ages.
