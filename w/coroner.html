    coroner

    /kˈɔrənɚ/ やや難 頻度 13445位

    意味

    1. 検死官

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English coroner, from Old French curuner, from Medieval Latin custōs placitōrum corōnae (“guardian of the crown's pleas”). The function was originally to protect royal properties. Compare crowner. Doublet of crown.
