    fellow

    /fˈɛloʊ/ 頻出 頻度 2248位

    要点男、やつ、人、仲間、仲間同士の

    意味

    1. 《話》男,やつ
    2. 《話》(一般に)人,だれしも(anybody)
    3. 《通例複数形で》仲間,同僚(comrade, companion)
    4. (学会などの)会員(member)
    5. 《英》大学の評議員(理事),《米》(大学の)特別研究員(給費(fellowship)を受けて研究に従事する大学
    6. 院生,研究員)
    7. (一対の物の)片方,相手(match, mate)
    8. 仲間同士の,同類の,同業の

    例文

    • He is quite a nice fellow and liked by everyone.

      彼はなかなかいいやつで、皆に好かれている。

      Tatoeba #292242

    • I absolutely will not speak to that fellow again!

      あいつとは断固２度と口をきかんぞ。

      Tatoeba #72047

    • The teacher was impatient with the idle fellow.

      先生はその怠け者に腹を立てていた。

      Tatoeba #272886

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English felawe, felowe (“companion, good friend”) from Old English fēolaga, from Old Norse félagi, derived from félag (“joint venture; partnership”, literally “a laying together of property”), from fé (“livestock, property; money”) (ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *peḱ- (“livestock; wealth”)) + lag (“something laid down; right position; arrangement; companionship, fellowship; partnership”) (ultimately from Proto-Indo-European *legʰ- (“to lie down”)).
