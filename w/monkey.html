    monkey（モンキー）

    /mˈʌŋki/ 頻出 頻度 2920位

    要点さる

    意味

    1. さる(猿)(普通小さくて尾のあるサル)
    2. 《話》猿に似た顔つきの人(動作をする人);(特に)いたずら小僧
    3. 《英俗》500ポンド,500ドル
    4. (…を)いじる《+around with+名》

    例文

    • Pan is a monkey that can spread butter on bread.

      パン君はパンにバターを塗れる猿です。

      Tatoeba #34939

    • The child was feeding the monkey with the banana.

      子供は猿にバナナを食べさせていた。

      Tatoeba #245658

    • The monkey took a banana by means of the stick.

      そのサルは棒を使ってバナナを取った。

      Tatoeba #50348

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Uncertain: * May be derived from monk + -ey (diminutive suffix), * or borrowed from Middle Low German Moneke, the name of the son of Martin the Ape in Reynard the Fox (which may represent an unattested colloquial Middle Low German *moneke, *moneken), itself of uncertain origin: ** Possibly derived from a Romance term represented by Late Middle French monne (whence Modern French mone (“monkey”)) or earlier Old French monnekin (“monkey”), originally Monnekin, the name of a monkey in Li Dis d'Entendement. Compare also Old French and Middle French monin (“monkey”).
