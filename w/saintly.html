    saintly

    /sˈeɪntli/ やや難 頻度 14080位

    意味

    1. 聖人らしい
    2. 気高い,崇高な

    語源

    From Middle English *saintli (suggested by seyntly (adverb)), equivalent to saint + -ly. Compare saintlike.
