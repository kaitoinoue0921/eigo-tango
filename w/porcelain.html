    porcelain

    /pˈɔrsələn/ やや難 頻度 7478位

    意味

    1. (材料としての)磁器
    2. 《集合的に》磁器製品

    例文

    • The Imari porcelain plate that my father cherished was quite heavy.

      祖父が大事にしていた伊万里焼の皿は、ずっしりと重かった。

      Tatoeba #2628991

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Borrowed from Middle French porcelaine (“cowrie, wampum; china, chinaware”), from Old Italian porcellana (“cowrie; china, chinaware”), from Italian porcello (“piglet”). The material was so called because of its resemblance to the shell of the cowrie. Why the cowrie was named with a word meaning “piglet” is unclear.
