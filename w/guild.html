    guild

    /ɡɪld/ やや難 頻度 13067位

    意味

    1. (中世の)ギルド
    2. (一般に)同業組合,組合,協会

    語源

    From Middle English gilde, from Old Norse gildi (“payment, guild”). Related to geld, yield, yauld.
