    cape

    /keɪp/ 標準 頻度 5659位

    要点みさき

    意味

    1. ケープ(婦人服の短いそでなし外とう)
    2. みさき(岬)
    3. 《the C-》=the Cape of Good Hope

    例文

    • Cape Dezhnev is 30 miles south of the Arctic Circle.

      デジネフ岬は北極圏の南３０マイルにある。

      Tatoeba #39465

    • My uncle lived alone in a secluded house on the cape.

      叔父さんは、岬の一軒家に独りぼっちで住んでいた。

      Tatoeba #11502061

    • There a cape pushes out into the sea.

      そこでは岬が海に突き出している。

      Tatoeba #51017

    語源

    From Middle English cape, gappe, cap, from Old French cap (“cape, headland”), from Latin caput (“head”). Compare Old Norse kobbi (“islet, small cape”). Doublet of capo, caput, chef, and chief, and distantly with head and Howth. For sense development, compare English ness (“a promontory point, cape”) from a root related to nose, Bulgarian нос (nos) (the same PIE root). Also compare coast (<< Latin costa).
