    slime

    /slaɪm/ やや難 頻度 9617位

    意味

    1. ねば土; (カタツムリ・魚などの)粘液, ぬめり; 嫌な物
      ねば土[粘液]を塗る[除く,で覆う]
      どろどろ[ぬるぬる,ねばねば]するもの; (川底などの)軟泥,へどろ

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    Etymology tree Proto-Indo-European *sley- Proto-Germanic *slīmą Old English slīm English slime From Middle English slime, slyme, slim, slym, from Old English slīm, from Proto-West Germanic *slīm, from Proto-Germanic *slīmą, from Proto-Indo-European *sley- (“smooth; slick; sticky; slimy”). Cognates include Saterland Frisian Sliem, Dutch slijm, German Schleim (“mucus, slime”), Danish, Norwegian Bokmål, and Norwegian Nynorsk slim, Swedish slem, Faroese and Icelandic slím (“slime”), Latin limus (“mud”), Ancient Greek λίμνη (límnē, “marsh”). The slang sense of "to murder" spread among Black communites throughout the 2010s and gained prominence on TikTok around 2021.
