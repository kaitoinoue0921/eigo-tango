    meanwhile

    /mˈinwˌaɪl/ 標準 頻度 4762位

    意味

    1. =meantime

    例文

    • Meanwhile, you can get a little sleep.

      それまで少し横になるといいよ。

      Tatoeba #9787307

    • Meanwhile, you can stay with us.

      それまで、僕たちと一緒にいるといいよ。

      Tatoeba #3714850

    • Meanwhile, a new year began.

      とかくするうちに年が明けた。

      Tatoeba #38282

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English menewhile, equivalent to mean (“intermediate”) + while. Adverb is by ellipsis from in the mean while, noun being conventionally written as one word after the adjective.
