    scarlet

    /skˈɑrlət/ やや難 頻度 9817位

    要点緋色、緋色の

    意味

    1. 緋色(ひいろ),深紅(しんく)色(あざやかな赤(bright red))
    2. 緋色の,深紅色の,まっかな

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English scarlet, scarlat, borrowed from Old French escarlate (“a type of cloth”), from Medieval Latin scarlātum (“scarlet cloth”), of uncertain origin. This was long thought to derive from Classical Persian سقرلات (saqirlāt, “a warm woollen cloth”), but the Persian word (first attested in the 1290s) is now thought to be from Arabic سِقِلَّات (siqillāt), denoting very expensive, luxury silks dyed scarlet-red using the exceptionally expensive dye, first attested around the ninth century.
