    subscription

    /səbskrˈɪpʃən/ やや難 頻度 6362位

    要点予約、寄付金

    意味

    1. (雑誌・切符などの)予約;(慈善事業などへの)寄付申込み《+to+名》
    2. (…への)寄付金,寄付《+to+名》
    3. (募金によって集められた)資金,寄金
    4. 《話》署名

    例文

    • Your subscription expires with the June issue.

      あなたの定期購読予約は六月号で切れます。

      Tatoeba #70458

    • Do you have a home newspaper subscription?

      家で新聞って、取ってる？

      Tatoeba #10724401

    • All subscriptions must be paid before the end of this year.

      予約購読料は今年末以前に全て払ってください。

      Tatoeba #324753

    語源

    From Latin subscriptiō; by surface analysis, sub- + script + -ion. All senses reflect (either literally or figuratively) the notion of signing one's own name beneath some promise or description to indicate that one agrees with it or agrees to it.
