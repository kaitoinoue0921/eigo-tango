    prognosis

    /prɑɡnˈoʊsəs/ やや難 頻度 10026位

    意味

    1. (病気の)予後(経過および結果の見通し)
    2. (一般に)予知,予測

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    The plural prognoses is a learned borrowing from Late Latin prognōsēs. Cognates * Middle French prognosie (“forecast, prediction”) (modern French prognosie (obsolete), prognose (“forecast of the course and outcome of a disease”)) * Sanskrit प्रज्ञा (prajñā, “intelligence, wisdom; judgment; knowledge”)
