    stratosphere

    /strˈætəsfˌɪr/ やや難 頻度 14102位

    意味

    1. 成層圏(地上約60kmまで広がる大気層)

    語源

    From French stratosphère, a word coined by its discoverer, meteorologist Léon Teisserenc de Bort. From strato- + -sphere.
