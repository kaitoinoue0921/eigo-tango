    slang

    /slæŋ/ やや難 頻度 6847位

    要点俗語、通語

    意味

    1. 俗語,スラング
    2. (特定集団の)通語,仲間言葉(jargon)
    3. 〈人〉の悪口を言う,〈人〉をののしる

    例文

    • It's fun to learn slang words in foreign languages.

      外国語のスラングを知るのは楽しい。

      Tatoeba #475757

    • Don't use slang if you can help it.

      できることなら俗語は使わないようにしなさい。

      Tatoeba #39563

    • The furry fandom, like many other fandoms, has its own slang.

      ケモナー界隈には他の多くの界隈と同様、独自のスラングがあります。

      Tatoeba #9337142

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    First use appears c. 1756, meaning "special vocabulary of tramps or thieves", origin unknown. Not believed to be connected with language or lingo. Possibly derived from a North Germanic source, then possibly related to Nordic language: Danish slænge, Icelandic and Norwegian Nynorsk slengja, Norwegian slenge, Swedish slänga (“to (carelessly) sling, throw, hurl; throw away, to dispose of; to flail”), with derivational nouns such as slæng, sleng, släng etc.
