    shipping

    /ʃˈɪpɪŋ/ 標準 頻度 5416位

    意味

    1. 船積み,積み出し,発送
    2. 海運業,運送業
    3. 《集合的に》(一港・一国・一海運会社などの)船舶;船舶トン数

    例文

    • It must have been broken during shipping.

      船積みの時壊れたに違いない。

      Tatoeba #273500

    • It must've been broken during shipping.

      船積みの時壊れたに違いない。

      Tatoeba #6681186

    • Would you please inform me of the expected shipping date?

      船積みの予定日をご連絡ください。

      Tatoeba #273501

    語源

    From Middle English schipping, schyppynge, from schippen, schipen (“to take ship, navigate”), from Old English scipian (“to take ship; put in order, equip, man a ship”), equivalent to ship + -ing.
