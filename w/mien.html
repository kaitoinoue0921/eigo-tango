    mien

    /min/ やや難 頻度 14457位

    意味

    1. (人柄・感情が分かるような)態度,物腰,様子

    語源

    From French mine (“appearance”) (whence also Danish mine and German Miene), perhaps from Breton min (“face of an animal”), or from Latin minio (“to redden”).
