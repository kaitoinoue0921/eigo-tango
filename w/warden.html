    warden

    /wˈɔrdən/ やや難 頻度 7371位

    意味

    1. (防火・猟区などの)管理人
    2. 《米》刑務所長
    3. 《英》学長

    例文

    • There was only one warden on duty when the riot started.

      暴動が起こったとき、当直の監視員はたった１人だった。

      Tatoeba #321200

    語源

    From Middle English wardein, from Anglo-Norman wardein, from warder (“to guard”), variant of Old French guarder (“to guard”) (whence modern French garder, also English guard), from Proto-Germanic *wardāną; related to Old High German wartēn (“to watch”). Compare guardian, French gardien, from Old French guardian, guardein. Compare also ward and reward. Doublet of guardian.
