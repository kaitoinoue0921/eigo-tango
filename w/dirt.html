    dirt

    /dɚt/ 頻出 頻度 2679位

    要点不潔な物

    意味

    1. 不潔な物,汚物;泥,ほこり,ごみ
    2. 土(soil)
    3. 無価値なもの
    4. わい談,春画,わいせつ本(pornography)
    5. 《話》卑わいな言葉;(特に)卑わいな悪口

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English drit (“excrement”), from Old Norse drit (“excrement”), from Proto-Germanic *dritą, *dritō (“excrement”), from Proto-Indo-European *dʰreyd-, *treydʰ- (“to have diarrhea”). Cognate with dialectal Danish and Norn drit (“excrement”), Norwegian dritt (“excrement”), dialectal Swedish dret (“shit”), Faroese and Icelandic drit (“bird excrement”), Dutch drijten (“to defecate”), drits (“dirt, mud, filth”), drijt and dreet (“excrement”), Low German drieten (“to defecate”), Driet (“shit”), regional German Driss (“shit”), Old English ġedrītan (“to defecate”). The word originally referred to excrement before shifting to the current sense of "soil".
