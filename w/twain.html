    twain

    /tweɪn/ やや難 頻度 8649位

    意味

    1. マーク・トウェイン(Mark~;1835‐1910;米国の小説家で,本名はSamuel Langhorne Clemens)
    2. 二つ(二人,2個;対)

    語源

    Etymology tree Proto-Indo-European *dwóh₁ Proto-Germanic *twai Proto-West Germanic *twai-der. Old English twēġen Middle English tweyne English twain PIE word *dwóh₁ From Middle English tweyne, tweien, twaine, from Old English twēġen m (“two”), from Proto-West Germanic *twai-, from Proto-Germanic *twai, from Proto-Indo-European *dwóh₁. Cognate with Saterland Frisian twäin, Low German twene, German zween. More at two. The word outlasted the breakdown of gender in Middle English and survived as a secondary form of two, then especially in the cases where the numeral follows a noun.
