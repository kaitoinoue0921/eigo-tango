    serpent

    /sˈɚpənt/ やや難 頻度 7818位

    要点ヘビ、蛇

    意味

    1. (特に大きくて毒を持った)ヘビ(蛇)
    2. 蛇に似た怪物(dragonなど)
    3. こうかつな人,陰険な人

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English serpent, from Old French serpent (“snake, serpent”), from Latin serpēns (“snake”), present active participle of serpere (“to creep, crawl”), from Proto-Italic *serpō, from Proto-Indo-European *serp-. In this sense, displaced native Old English nǣdre (“snake, serpent”), whence Modern English adder. Compare Sanskrit सर्प (sarpa, “snake”), which is a descendant of the same Proto-Indo-European word as serpent.
