    lobe

    /loʊb/ やや難 頻度 13796位

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle French lobe in early 16th century, from New Latin lobus (“a lobe”), from Ancient Greek λοβός (lobós, “the lobe of the ear or of the liver, the pod of a leguminous plant”).
