    human

    /hjˈumən/ 最頻出 頻度 555位

    要点人間の、人間らいし

    意味

    1. (動物・神に対して)人間の,人の
    2. 人間らいし,人間的な,人情味のある
    3. 《複数形》(動物に怠して)人間(human being)
    4. 《the human》人類

    例文

    • Humans are the only animals capable of laughter.

      笑うことができる動物は人間だけです。

      Tatoeba #9484642

    • Language is a specifically human characteristic.

      言語は人間固有の性質である。

      Tatoeba #239738

    • In humans, the eyes act together with the brain.

      人間の目は脳と共に働く。

      Tatoeba #270226

    Etymology tree Latin hūmānusbor. Old French umain Middle French humainbor. Middle English humayne English human From Late Middle English humaigne, humayne, humain, from Middle French humain, from Old French humain, umain (“of or belonging to man”, adjective), from Latin hūmānus m (“of man, human”, adjective); see there for further details. Spelling human has been predominant since the early 18th century. Compare, and possibly related to man.
