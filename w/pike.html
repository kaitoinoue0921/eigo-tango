    pike

    /paɪk/ やや難 頻度 10727位

    意味

    1. やり,ほこ(17世紀末まで歩兵が武器として用いた)
    2. (やり・ほこの)…を突き刺す
    3. カワカマス
    4. =turnpike

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English pyke, pyk, pik, pike (“pike; sharp point, iron tip of a staff or spear, pointed toe of an item of footwear; sharp tool; mountain, peak”), from Old English pīc (“pointed object, pick axe”), and Middle French pique (“long thrusting weapon”), from Old French pic (“sharp point, spike”); both ultimately from Proto-West Germanic *pīk, from Proto-Germanic *pīkaz, *pīkō (“sharp point, pike, peak”), related to pick with a narrower meaning. The word is cognate with Middle Dutch pecke, peke, picke (modern Dutch piek), German Pike, Norwegian pik, Danish pig, and possibly Old Irish pīk. It is a doublet of pique.
