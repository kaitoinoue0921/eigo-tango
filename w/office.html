    officeオフィス

    /ˈɔfɪs/ 最頻出 頻度 371位

    要点事務所、公職、官庁

    意味

    1. 事務所,事務室;営業所,会社
    2. 《集合的に》《単数扱い》事務所(営業所)の全員
    3. (特に政府・会社などの)公職,官職,職務
    4. (一般に)任務,務め,役目(duty)
    5. 官庁,役所;《O-》《米》局,《英》省
    6. 《複数形で》(…の)尽力,親切,世話《+of+名》
    7. 《時にO-》(宗教上の)儀式,葬儀;(定められた時間の)礼拝

    語源

    英語の原文（Wiktionary）

    From Middle English office, from Old French office, from Latin officium (“personal, official, or moral duty; official position; function; ceremony, esp. last rites”), contracted from opificium (“construction: the act of building or the thing built”), from opifex (“doer of work, craftsman”) + -ium (“-y”, forming actions), from op- (“work”) + -i- (connective) + -fex (combining form of faciō (“to do, to make”)). In the religious sense, Displaced native Old English hād. The computing sense is a genericization of various proprietary program suites, such as Microsoft Office.
